tree·of·life patent browser

Pick a taxon (any rank — order, family, genus, species) and optionally a CPC subclass. The tree shows descendant species linked to at least one patent in that CPC. Hover a leaf to see the actual patent titles; click to open them on Google Patents.

    Graph: 4.53M taxa · 759k patents · 35k CPC classes Code: github.ugent.be/wvcrieki/treeoflife
